# Play Submission Files

This folder contains draft files required for Google Play submission.

## Files
- `PRIVACY_POLICY.md`: source privacy policy text.
- `privacy_policy.html`: web-hostable privacy policy page.
- `DATA_SAFETY_FORM_DRAFT.md`: draft answers for Play Data Safety form.
- `PLAY_CONTENT_FORMS_DRAFT.md`: draft answers for Play App Content forms.
- `SMS_PERMISSION_COMPLIANCE.md`: SMS policy risk analysis and declaration strategy.

## Before Submission
- Replace placeholder contact values in the privacy policy.
- Host `privacy_policy.html` publicly and use that URL in Play Console.
- Verify Data Safety answers against final app behavior and permissions.
- Complete Play declarations for sensitive permissions.
