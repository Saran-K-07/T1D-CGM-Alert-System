Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-05

This Privacy Policy explains how T1D Alert (“we”, “our”, “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the app.

Information We Collect

  • Precise location (including background access): Used only during low SGV/fall events to include coordinates in emergency SMS alerts.
  • SMS data: Emergency alert messages sent to user‑configured trusted contacts when the alert system is active.
  • App settings: Your configured contacts and alert preferences stored locally on your device.

How We Use Information

  • To send emergency SMS alerts during low SGV or fall events.
  • To include precise coordinates in the alert for faster assistance.
  • To operate core app features based on your settings.

How We Share Information

  • Trusted contacts only: We share location and alert messages only via SMS to contacts you choose.
  • No servers: We do not upload or store your location or SMS data on any server.

Data Storage and Retention

We store alert settings and trusted contacts locally on your device. We do not retain location data beyond what is needed to send an alert.

Your Choices and Controls

  • You can disable SMS alerts or location access in app settings.
  • You can manage or remove trusted contacts at any time.
  • You can revoke permissions in device settings.

Permissions We Request

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION to include location in emergency alerts.
  • SEND_SMS and RECEIVE_SMS for emergency alert messaging.

Children’s Privacy

This app is intended for children under 13. We require parental consent before use. We do not collect personal data beyond location (during emergency events) and SMS alerts to trusted contacts, and we do not use analytics or advertising SDKs.

Medical Disclaimer

This app is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Alerts may be inaccurate or delayed. Always seek professional medical guidance.

Open Source

The app’s source code may be publicly available, but we do not transmit or store your personal data on any server.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the “Last updated” date.
